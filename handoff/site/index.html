FAQ

Still have a question? Ask us directly.

What is CacheDrop?

CacheDrop is a native Mac storage cleaner for developers and video editors. It finds hidden caches, Xcode DerivedData, old node_modules folders and video render files, shows how much space each one takes, and clears what you approve.

How do I free up space on my Mac?

Start with the files you can’t see. Caches, build files and render data can take tens of gigabytes, and macOS hides them inside “System Data”. CacheDrop scans for them, lets you review what it found, and clears it in one click.

What is System Data on a Mac, and can I delete it?

System Data is where macOS counts files that don’t fit its other categories, including app caches, logs and developer build files. Much of it is safe to remove because apps rebuild it. CacheDrop only targets those rebuildable parts.

Is it safe to delete caches on a Mac?

Yes, for caches that apps regenerate: the app simply rebuilds them the next time it needs them. CacheDrop only recommends rebuildable caches and shows you each one before anything moves.

How do I clear my Mac’s disk without losing files?

Clear caches and build output, not documents. CacheDrop leaves your code, projects and timelines alone, and sends what it clears to the Trash.

Is CacheDrop free, and which Macs does it support?

It’s free for a limited time during the public beta. It’s a universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
