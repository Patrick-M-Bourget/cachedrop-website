# SectionHeader

Eyebrow, heading and lede for the top of a section.

**Provide** `title`; optional `eyebrow`, `lede`, `level` (heading level, default 2), `size` (`lg` homepage sections, `md` guide sections, `xl` closing CTA) and `align` (`start` default, `center` for CTA bands only).

- Headings are statements that end with a period on marketing pages (“Built for the heavy lifters.”) and plain questions on guides (“What is eating your Mac’s storage?”).
- The lede is one or two sentences, max 620px, and is where the section’s keyword goes.
- Keep a strict H1 → H2 → H3 order: one H1 per page; section headers are H2.
